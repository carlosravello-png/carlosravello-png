I design the semantic infrastructure that determines what Google, LLMs, and the right people decide you are.

I build and operate my own ecosystems — a precision cycling laboratory, an international veterinary clinic, a Japanese street food concept — each grounded in the same methodology: documented accuracy or it doesn't exist.

That methodology has a name: the **Dynamic Coherence Model (MCD)** — a framework for identity architecture, decision-making consistency, and semantic brand structure. It is the system that makes everything I build coherent, and the frame from which I design for others when the case merits it.

My research is published under verifiable DOI and indexed in Zenodo and OpenAIRE.
